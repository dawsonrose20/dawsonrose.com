About

I've been using PCs since I was a kid, and I've always wanted to know how they actually work.

Heading into my senior year of high school, I wasn't sure what I wanted to do. A short stint in the robotics club settled it: I wanted to spend college building the hardware and code behind the machines I'd grown up with.

Electrical and computer engineering feels like the field most visibly at the front of human progress and innovation, and that's where I want to be. At Northeastern I'm doing that through hands-on builds: assistive devices, arcade machines, escape-room puzzles, and now circuit boards with Northeastern Electric Racing.

Off the clock: snowboarding · leatherworking · golf · film
